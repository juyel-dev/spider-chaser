# Privacy Policy — Spider Chaser

_Last updated: October 2026_

Spider Chaser is a browser extension that shows animated spiders on web pages.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** The extension does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or browsing data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What stays on your device
The extension saves your preferences (speed, size, number of spiders, on/off switches and the list of websites where you turned the spiders off) in your browser's local extension storage. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the extension.

## Permissions
- **Storage** – to save the preferences above.
- **Active tab** – to read the address (hostname) of the tab you are looking at when you open the popup, so it can show the site name and the "Disable on this site" button. The address is not stored anywhere except, if you choose to disable a site, the hostname you disabled in your local preference list.
- **Access to web pages** – the spiders are drawn on top of the pages you visit. The extension does not read, change or transmit the content of those pages, what you type, or your browsing history.

## Network
The extension makes no network requests. All code is included in the package; no remote code is loaded.

## Children
The extension does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same location with a new date.
