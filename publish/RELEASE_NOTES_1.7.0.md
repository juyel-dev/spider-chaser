## Spider Chaser 1.7.0

**New**
- 🦋 A colourful **butterfly** (monarch, blue, yellow or purple) flutters around the page on its own: it flaps, glides, sometimes lands and slowly opens and closes its wings, keeps away from the spiders, and flies off after a while. Switch it off in the popup.
- Webs are now drawn as clean straight threads, spoke by spoke and ring by ring, instead of following the spider's tail
- Popup: new **Butterfly** switch; the four switches (Drops, Flies, Webs, Butterfly) sit in a 2×2 grid
- Screenshots of every behaviour in the README, generated by `test/shoot.js`

**Fixed / improved**
- Calmer walking: lower top speed and shorter steps, so the legs no longer fold into sharp angles when a spider runs
- The silk thread is now a thin crisp line (it was a blurry band)
- Spiders keep a bit more distance from each other
- Added CI (syntax check + 10-minute headless simulation) and a Screenshots workflow

**Install**
1. Download `spider-chaser-1.7.0.zip` below and unzip it
2. Open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode**
3. **Load unpacked** → choose the unzipped folder
4. Refresh any open page

No data is collected and there are no network requests. See [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/juyel-dev/spider-chaser/blob/main/publish/PRIVACY.md).
